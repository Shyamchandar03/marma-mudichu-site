Marma Mudichu

மர்ம முடிச்சு · Tamil interactive mystery stories

Marma Mudichu is a YouTube and Instagram channel that posts a short, original Tamil detective story every day. Each episode ends with a question, and viewers help the investigator Nizhal solve the case in the comments. The answer, along with the names of correct solvers, is revealed in the next episode.

The channel uses a small private automation tool, run by the channel owner, to prepare and upload its own videos to its own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.
